• The structure preview window allows interactive visualisation of the complex structure of the designed binder (orange) and the protein target (blue) predicted by AlphaFold2 “initial guess”.
  • Different binders can be previewed by selecting the corresponding row in the results summary table.

SBP only keeps designed binders that pass the predefined filters. Some runs will generate no outputs that meet the filtering criteria and will not return any output files. To overcome this limitation, you can try different Target Hotspot Residues combinations or increase the Number of Designs.

Does the designed binder look like a globular protein?

While the automated pipeline can produce binders that meet in silico quality control criteria, it is worth looking at the predicted structures to perform some manual assessment. Outputs will often be globular helical bundles, but the pipeline (particularly RFdiffusion) can occasionally produce elongated, isolated helices which are likely poor candidates for experimental study.

Does the designed binder target the desired epitope?

While the model is encouraged to design binders in the vicinity of user-supplied hotspots, this requirement is not enforced during design. Binders can often ‘escape’ to privileged hydrophobic patches which may not lead to biologically relevant candidates (particularly for transmembrane targets). You can check the predicted complex structure to ensure the binder appears at the intended site.

During the design process, the target structure is renumbered from 1. If the input structure was not numbered from 1, the input residue hotspots will appear at a different residue index in the output target-binder complex.

  • The results summary table contains an overview of designed binders that passed the SBP quality control filters (PAE interaction < 10 Å, pLDDT > 80, and binder RMSD < 1 Å).
  • The total number of ranked designs passing the design filters is described in the table header (16 in the example above).
  • Results are ranked by the PAE interaction score.

Interpreting Your Results

De novo design result definitions
Column Description
Rank The rank of the designed binder based on PAE interaction score.
PAE interaction The bidirectional mean cross-chain PAE between all binder and target residue pairs, used as the primary measure of binder confidence. Lower is better.
ipTM TM-score-like confidence score based on a normalised PAE used as an alternative measure of binder confidence (closely related to PAE interaction). Higher is better.
Design Length The length of the designed binder.
Design Sequence The amino acid sequence of the designed binder.
Design Name The identifier of the designed binder.

Any binder that passes the SBP quality control filters could be considered a plausible candidate for experimental evaluation.

Structure prediction confidence scores (such as PAE interaction and ipTM) are widely used as discrete filters, but once above the passing threshold, scores are not found to correlate with binding affinity or success rate (Adaptyv blog (opens in a new tab)).
