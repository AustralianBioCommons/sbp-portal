• The structure preview window allows interactive visualisation of the predicted structure.
  • Structures are coloured as Query (orange) or Target (blue) proteins.
  • Different structures can be previewed by selecting the corresponding row in the results summary table.
  • The preview window can be resized to increase/decrease the viewable area.

Structure prediction models were designed to predict the most likely structure of a complex under the assumption that the components are known to interact. In that setting, predictions have been shown to closely match experimental structures. Using model confidence as a proxy to identify previously unknown protein complexes has been less well validated and results should be interpreted carefully.

Is the identified interaction biologically plausible?

When evaluating putative novel complexes, care should be taken to consider the biological context. When considering a transmembrane query, it is unlikely for a true partner to be located at a membrane-embedded site with an orientation perpendicular to the membrane. Similarly, a cytoplasmic protein may be less likely to form a bona fide interaction with an extracellular domain.

  • The total number of predicted interactions is described in the table header (999 here).
  • Query ID is the identifier for the interaction component derived from the FASTA header of the input Query Sequences.
  • Target ID is the identifier for the interaction component derived from the FASTA header of the input Target Sequences.
  • ipSAE is a score representing the confidence in the arrangement of high-confidence regions in the predicted complex.
  • ipTM is a score representing the confidence in the global arrangement of subunits in the predicted complex. pTM is the same score but includes confidence in the intra-chain arrangement.

See example data for the single prediction workflow for a detailed description of structure prediction confidence scores.

Interpreting Your Results

  1. Scenario 1: 🟢 High ipSAE & 🟢 High ipTM

    In this scenario, the model is highly confident in every aspect of the structure prediction. This predicted complex is likely high quality.

  2. Scenario 2: 🟢 High ipSAE & 🔴 Low ipTM

    High ipSAE alone supports a well-defined prediction interface. While the ipTM score can be deflated by long flexible loops which do not participate in the interaction, ipSAE was designed to overcome this limitation by excluding low-confidence regions and applying appropriate normalisation.

The AlphaFoldDB has incorporated predicted viral complexes derived from a blind interaction screening workflow. A cutoff of ipSAE > 0.6 was used to minimise the chance of false positives.
